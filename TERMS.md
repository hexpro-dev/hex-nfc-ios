## 6. NFC tags are physical hardware

Writing to a tag changes a physical chip, and some changes cannot be undone:

- Permanently locking a tag is irreversible. Certain formatting and
  configuration changes are also one-way on some chips.
- Password-protecting a tag makes it unreadable or unwritable without the
  password. Keep track of passwords you set: if the stored copy is lost, the
  tag may become permanently inaccessible.
- Chip behaviour varies between manufacturers and production batches. The app
  takes extensive precautions, but a write can still fail or leave a tag
  unusable.

Only scan or write tags you own or have the owner's permission to modify. You
are responsible for what you write to a tag and for where you leave it. To the
extent the law allows, we are not responsible for tags that end up locked,
damaged, or unusable, or for data lost from a tag.

## 7. Passports and identity documents

The passport feature reads the chip in electronic travel documents and displays

Read only documents that belong to you, or documents you have the holder's
clear permission to read, and always comply with the law where you are.

The integrity indicator checks that the chip's data files are internally
consistent. It does not verify the document against government certificates,
