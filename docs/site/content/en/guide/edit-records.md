A tag holds a list of records, and Hex NFC shows that list on the Tag screen.
Add one, change one, move one up the list or delete one, then write the tag from
your iPhone once the list reads the way you want it.

## Open the record list

Do either of the following:

- To work on a tag you have in your hand, scan it. Its records appear on the Tag
  screen. See [Scan a tag](scan-a-tag.md).
- To build a set of records with no tag in front of you, tap **Build** on the
  home screen.

A tag or a build that holds nothing yet reads "Empty tag", with
"Add a record to get started." underneath.

## Add a record

::::steps
:::step[Open the record picker]
On the Tag screen, tap **Add record**. It sits at the end of the record list, or
in the middle of the screen when the tag holds nothing. The Add record screen
opens with one tile for each type you can add.
:::

:::step[Choose a type]
Tap the tile you want, such as **Text**, **URL**, **Contact** or **Wi-Fi**. The
app adds a record of that type and opens it for you to fill in.
:::

:::step[Fill in the record]
Enter the details the record needs, then tap **Save**. The record appears at the
end of the list on the Tag screen.
:::
::::

There are 13 types to choose from. [Record types](record-types.md) covers what
each one holds and what a phone does with it when it reads the tag.

## Add a record from a QR code

::::steps
:::step[Open the QR reader]
On the Add record screen, tap **Scan QR**. The Scan a QR code screen opens.
:::

:::step[Allow the camera]
The first time, the screen reads "Allow camera to scan a QR" and
"The frame is processed on-device. Nothing is uploaded." Tap **Allow camera**,
then allow access when iOS asks. If you refused the camera earlier, the screen
reads "Camera access needed" instead. Tap **Open Settings** and turn the camera
back on for Hex NFC.
:::

:::step[Point the camera at the code]
Hold the code inside the frame. The app reads the first code it sees, turns it
into a record, and opens it for you to check.
:::
::::

The type it picks matches what the code carries: a Wi-Fi network, a contact, an
email, a text message, a phone number or a location. A web address becomes a URL
record. Anything else becomes a text record.

## Change, move or delete a record

Do any of the following on the Tag screen:

- To change a record, tap its row, edit the fields, then tap **Save**.
- To move a record, drag its row up or down. The list order is the order the
  records are written to the tag.
- To delete a record, swipe left on its row and tap **Delete**. Swiping the row
  all the way across deletes it without that second tap.

A record you delete here is gone from the list, not from the tag. The tag keeps
what it had until the next write, so a list you emptied by mistake costs you
nothing. To clear a tag for real, see
[Erase or format a tag](erase-or-format.md).

## Finish the edit

Nothing on this screen reaches the tag until you write it. Once the list is
right, do one of the following:

- To put the records on the tag, tap **Write**. See
  [Write a link to a tag](write-a-link.md).
- To keep the set for another day, open the menu at the top of the Tag screen
  and tap **Save as preset…**. See [Save a tag as a preset](presets.md).
- To leave, tap **Close**. Your tag stays as it was, and edits you neither wrote
  nor saved are dropped.
