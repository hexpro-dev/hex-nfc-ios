Hex NFC keeps a copy of the tags it reads, so you can open a scan again with
the tag nowhere near you. Your recent scans sit in the Library on your iPhone,
newest first. Pin the ones you want to keep.

## Open a scan you made before

You need a tag you have already read; until you scan one, **History** is empty.
See [Scan a tag](scan-a-tag.md).

::::steps
:::step[Open Hex NFC]
The app opens on the home screen, which carries the **Scan**, **Build**,
**Library** and **Advanced** tiles.
:::

:::step[Open the Library]
Tap **Library**. The Library screen opens on **Presets**.
:::

:::step[Go to your scans]
At the top of the Library screen, tap **History**, to the right of
**Presets**. Each row is headed by the chip's name, with the tag's UID and
how long ago you read it underneath.
:::

:::step[Open the scan]
Tap the scan you want. The Library closes and the scan opens in the Tag screen,
holding the records and the chip details from the tap that made it.
:::
::::

A transit card opens in the Transit card screen instead, read-only, the way it
opened when you read the card. A passport read is never saved, so no passport
appears in this list. See [Read a passport](passports.md).

## Pin a scan you want to keep

The list holds 50 scans. When a new scan arrives and the list is full, the
oldest unpinned scan drops off the end. An unpinned scan also goes 90 days
after the read that made it, however short the list is. Where a re-scan drops
back onto a row you already have, that counts as a fresh read and the 90 days
start again. A pinned scan is outside both rules and stays until you delete it.

Scans are listed newest first and nothing on the screen searches them, so pin
the ones you will want to find again.

- To pin a scan, swipe right on it in **History**, then tap **Pin**.

A pinned scan carries a pin at the left of its row in place of the usual icon.
It keeps its place in the list rather than moving to the top. On a scan that is
already pinned the same swipe action reads **Unpin**, and unpinning puts the
scan back in the count of 50.

## Delete a scan

- To delete a scan, swipe left on it in **History**, then tap **Delete**.
  Swiping all the way across deletes the scan straight away.

:::warning
The scan goes the moment you tap **Delete**. Nothing asks you to confirm, and
there is no way back to it, although reading the tag again makes a fresh scan.
:::

## What the history holds

Before you read anything, **History** reads "No scan history", over the line
"The last 50 tags you scan show up here. Pin one to keep it."

- Read the same tag again. If nothing about the tap has changed, the scan you
  already have moves back to the top of the list. No second row appears.
  Anything different in the chip details, the records or the tag's password
  protection gives you a new row instead.
- Scans stay on this iPhone. They reach your iCloud account only when you turn
  on **iCloud sync**, which is off to start with. See
  [Privacy and your data](privacy.md).
