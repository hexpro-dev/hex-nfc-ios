---
title: Two taps and a typed UID
---

A change to a tag's protection holds the tag twice, and so does every operation
under **Dangerous Actions**. The first tap reads the tag's UID and closes
straight away, so you can type. The app then asks you to type that UID back,
character for character, and the second tap makes the change on the tag you
confirmed. Present a different tag on the second tap and the app refuses it
rather than changing the wrong one.
